NEW

Jaly Lodge

Press J in game. Click the tree, mirror, fireplace and presents to open mods, cosmetics, packs and daily gifts.

Sky Lyrics & Now Playing

See your Spotify song and its lyrics on your HUD, or floating in the sky. Turn them on in Mods > Music.

Two Christmas packs

Jaly Christmas is on by default: candy cane swords and a festive hotbar. Winter Wonderland turns the world snowy.

Profiles

Each profile is its own Minecraft version with its own mods, packs, worlds and settings. Pick one and press Play.

⭐ = JalyClient runs on this version (Lodge, cosmetics, built-in mods). Other versions get Fabric + any mods you pick, and JalyClient itself is coming to them.

Pre Installs

The mods JalyClient picks and keeps updated for you. Changes apply the next time you press Play.

Built into JalyClient

Mods

Get anything from Modrinth for your profile. You can do this in game too: press J and poke the grumpy guy by the fireplace.

What's new in JalyClient.

Version 1.7.0

Visuals, PvP tools and custom music discs

  • New Visuals tab in Tools: Better Sounds, Tall Smooth Grass, Better Ambience, Better Rain, Better Glass and Interactive Blocks
  • Better Sounds: wind, rain on the roof, birds, crickets, owls and cave drips
  • Tall Smooth Grass sways in the wind and blows harder in rain and storms (or freeze it for less lag)
  • Better Ambience: aurora borealis on some nights, shooting stars and glowing sunsets
  • Better Rain: realistic or blocky rain right in front of you, splashes and drops on your view
  • Better Glass: rain runs down your windows and frost creeps in when it's freezing
  • Interactive Blocks: grass wears into paths and plants get trampled in your singleplayer worlds
  • New PvP tools: Hit Markers, Kill Counter, Totem Pops, Item Cooldowns and Armor Break Warning
  • Custom Music Discs: press H, give it a song file or link, name it, pick a disc look, and play it in any jukebox
  • 4 new animated menu backgrounds: Frozen Lake, Blizzard Pines, Fireworks Night and Gingerbread Town
  • 2 new logos (Frosty and Gingerbread) and 6 new animated buttons
Version 1.6.0

Friends and walking pets

  • Friends list: add JalyClient players, see their status and server, join them in one click
  • Set yourself to Online, Do Not Disturb, Sleeping or Appear Offline
  • Your first friend unlocks the Ginger Buddy pet
  • Pets now walk beside you on the floor with their own animations; flying pets flap their wings
  • New pets: Baby Frost Dragon and Snowy Owl
  • Your skin spins in Cosmetics even from the main menu
  • Poke the grumpy guy three times and he asks what you want: Mods or Friends
  • Mod and resource pack icons in the in-game Mods tab; Pre Installs are now called Tools in game

  • JalyClient runs on 1.21 and 1.21.1; other versions get Fabric + your own mods for now
